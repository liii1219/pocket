Pocket

Exercise in the gaps of a working day. Say how many minutes you have, get one short session that fits.

Try the MVP opens instantly

One screen, two choices, no camera. A single HTML file, 141 lines.

Try the full version uses your camera

Counts your reps and corrects your form. Tell it whether anyone is around and it only offers movements that read as stretching. The pose model runs in your browser, so no video is uploaded.

Built for DECO2850 Interaction Design at the University of Queensland. Source code on GitHub.
